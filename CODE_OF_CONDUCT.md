# Community conduct

[Deutsch](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.de.md)

Discuss the work respectfully. Technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing and deliberate publication of another person's secrets are not.

Use reproducible examples, acknowledge uncertainty and focus criticism on code and behavior rather
than personal characteristics. Respect maintainers' time and contributors' boundaries.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a contact route the owner has
actually published. Do not disclose personal information in a public issue. The owner may remove
content, close discussions or restrict participation to protect the community. This is a project
policy, not a promise of continuous moderation or a legal dispute-resolution service.
